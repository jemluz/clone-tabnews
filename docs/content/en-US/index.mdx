# What is Tabnews?

The [TabNews](https://www.tabnews.com.br/) is a website forum focused on the technology community, aimed at debates and exchanging knowledge through publications and comments created by the users themselves. Its contents include articles, news, tutorials and more.

![Screenshot of a TabNews post about generating images with Stable Diffusion on Windows, illustrated with AI-generated images](/img/index/01.png)

## And why is that a big deal?

As it is a real project, it's a huge experience in software engineering. Dealing with large volumes of data such as

**→** `180K`` ``visitors` **→** `900K views` **→** `80K publications`

#### All with around 30K lines of code, from zero to production!

## Real life project with real life metrics

Three months after TabNews's official launch, **500K people were already visiting** it, totaling **3 million views** of its content.

![Vercel analytics dashboard showing 558,919 visitors and 2,952,245 page views](/img/index/04.png)

![Chart of TabNews page views in the Vercel analytics dashboard](/img/index/05.png)

With 27K people registered and 8.2K publications and 34K responses/interactions, forming one of the friendliest and most engaged communities on the web.

![Charts showing 27,000 new accounts, 8,200 posts, and 34,000 replies](/img/index/06.png)

---

In addition to the support of **50 contributors** in the open source project repository. And with **3 thousand stars on GitHub**.

![Mosaic of TabNews contributors' photos and avatars](/img/index/07.png)

![Chart showing the historical growth of stars on the TabNews GitHub repository](/img/index/08.png)

---

And it also ranked very well in Google analytics, reaching **almost 60K clicks in 30 days**, a jump of 24K clicks compared to the previous period.

![Google Search Console dashboard showing the 50,000-click milestone and progress toward 60,000 clicks](/img/index/09.png)

![Google Search Console card showing 52,200 of 60,000 clicks and an increase of 24,500 over the previous period](/img/index/10.png)

The Google console itself reveals a **growing trend of exposure of content created by Tabnews users**, being exposed even for super **competitive themes such as “GPT chat”** which has already had more than **1M impressions in searches**.

![Search Console chart showing 131,000 clicks, 4.51 million impressions, a 2.9% click-through rate, and an average position of 16.7](/img/index/11.png)
